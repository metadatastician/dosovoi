# Provisional cultural review framework

“DOSovoi” is wordplay combining DOS and Domovoi. Domovoi belongs to Slavic
folklore; this software does not present an authoritative Domovoi, reenact a
belief, or claim permission to universalise that tradition.

The bunny-like, cloud/puff, and tool-free attentive forms are provisional
prototype choices. The attentive form retains the internal identifier `lens`
for compatibility, but the inspection-like object was removed after internal
review. No creature, object, colour, symbol, or posture is assumed culturally
neutral. Their interpretations are unknown until reviewed with people from the
relevant cultures and contexts.

## Current constraints

- No prayer, blessing, judgement, divine protection, supernatural authority,
  religious iconography, national costume, caricature, or hand gesture.
- Postures are limited to resting, compact focus, facing a labelled warning,
  and low-stimulation stillness.
- Text avoids sentience, need, dependence, protection, authority, and safety
  knowledge.
- ASCII and plain-text alternatives avoid reliance on a single symbol set.

## Review prompts

For each proposed name, form, colour, object, posture, and phrase, ask:

1. What meanings arise in the reviewer’s own context, and how certain are they?
2. Does it trivialise, appropriate, caricature, infantilise, or imply authority?
3. Does it resemble religious, political, national, disability, or harmful
   symbolism?
4. Could a tool-bearing figure falsely imply inspection or expertise?
5. Does plain text alter or remove the concern?
6. Should the element be changed, made configurable, regionally withheld, or
   removed?

Record reviewer context with consent, dissent, uncertainties, changes, and the
date/scope of review. Do not turn one person into a representative of an entire
culture. Provide a private as well as public route for reporting discomfort or
offence before any wider evaluation.
